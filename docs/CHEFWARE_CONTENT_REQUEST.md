# ChefWare Website Content Request

## Why we need this

The website needs real ChefWare evidence: actual products, people, completed work, and the company story. This makes the brand credible and gives buyers confidence to order or request a quote.

Please send the items below in one Google Drive folder, WeTransfer link, or WhatsApp document bundle. Original files are best; do not send screenshots of images where the original photo is available.

## 1. Product photography — highest priority

For each product or product group, send 3–5 clear photos:

- Chef uniforms: front, back, fabric/finish detail, and a person wearing it.
- Restaurant and staff uniforms: full uniform, embroidery/branding close-up, and a team photo where possible.
- Kitchen equipment and tools: front, side, controls/details, scale in a real kitchen, and model/brand label.
- Customized machines: the completed machine, a relevant detail, and it being used where permission allows.

Photo standard: bright natural or studio lighting, clean background, no WhatsApp compression, and portrait plus landscape versions when possible. Minimum 1600px on the longest side. Please name files clearly, for example `chef-jacket-green-front.jpg` or `six-burner-range-control-panel.jpg`.

For every product group, provide: product name, brand/model, key specifications, available sizes/capacity, colour options, price or `quote only`, stock status, warranty terms, and realistic delivery/lead time.

## 2. Real work and service evidence

Please send photos and short notes for work ChefWare has actually completed: kitchen setup, installation, embroidery, logo printing, custom machine work, uniform supply, or delivery.

For each completed job, provide:

- Service delivered and a 2–3 sentence description of the problem and solution.
- Before/during/after photos where available.
- Client business name and location only if we have written permission to publish it.
- Completion month/year and scale of work, for example uniform quantity, kitchen size, or equipment supplied.
- A named contact, title, and approved testimonial only if the person/client has explicitly approved website use.

Do not send client names, logos, interior images, or testimonials that have not been approved for public use.

## 3. Team and leadership photography

Please send professional, current photos of:

- CEO/founder: one clean headshot and one natural working portrait.
- Key workers: equipment, embroidery/branding, delivery/operations, and customer-facing staff.
- Team at work: consultations, product preparation, embroidery, installation, quality checks, and delivery.

Avoid heavily filtered photos, crowded backgrounds, and images of people who have not agreed to be featured. For every person shown prominently, provide their preferred name, role, and approval to use the image on the website.

## 4. About ChefWare — written information

Please send answers in simple notes or a voice note:

- Year founded, founder(s), and why ChefWare was started.
- The business problem ChefWare exists to solve.
- Main customer types and geographic areas served.
- Exact products and services currently offered.
- What makes ChefWare different from alternatives.
- Key milestones, major projects, partnerships, certifications, awards, or supplier relationships that can be published.
- The CEO/founder's preferred 80–120 word biography and title.

We will turn this into a concise About Us page. Please confirm every factual claim before it goes live.

## 5. Brand, contact, and policy details

Please confirm:

- Official company name, logo files, brand colours, and any brand guidelines.
- Active WhatsApp number, phone number, email address, showroom/office address, hours, and social-media links.
- Delivery areas, delivery fees/free-delivery threshold, and realistic delivery timeframes.
- Warranty terms by product type, returns/exchange policy, payment methods, VAT treatment, and terms/privacy text or the contact who can approve them.

## 6. Best way to send it

Create folders named `01-products`, `02-completed-work`, `03-team`, `04-ceo`, `05-about-and-approvals`, and `06-brand-and-contact`. Include a short document named `content-notes` with filenames, product details, and approval notes.

The first delivery should prioritise: 10–20 strongest product photos, 3 completed jobs with approval, 3 team/CEO photos, and the About Us answers. That is enough to replace generic homepage imagery and begin the About Us page.
